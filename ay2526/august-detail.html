August Events Highlight

Welcome Week AY25/26

The Rotaract Club of NTU kicked off AY25/26 with our first event at LT4! Through our Welcome Week booth, students of NTU were given opportunities to discover more about our vibrant club, our activities, and how they could be a part of the meaningful initiatives we organise together.


Welcome Tea

At Wee Cho Yaw Plaza Auditorium, our Welcome Tea gave students a chance to chat with us. From learning about our different divisions to speaking directly with the directors, it was a wonderful evening of conversations, laughter, and sparking new connections.


Leadership Catalyst Bootcamp

Our Leadership Catalyst Bootcamp brought EXCO members and Directors together for a day of growth and bonding. With Prof. Kumar as our guest speaker, participants engaged in a workshop and case studies on leadership, enhancing their communication, problem-solving, and teamwork skills. The event was filled with learning, inspiration, and stronger connections across divisions.
